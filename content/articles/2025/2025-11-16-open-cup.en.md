title: 20th Carrom Open Cup Prague 2025

perex: We celebrated the 20th edition of the biggest carrom tournament in the Czech Republic!
---

The tournament took place on Saturday 15 November 2025 in Prague, with a
great atmosphere and players from abroad.

**Pawel Wroczynski** (PL) won, beating Maghesh Babu (CH) in the final.
The best Czech player was Karlos Novák, who became Czech champion for
the fourth year in a row.

Thanks to Prague 4 for its support, and to everyone who came.
