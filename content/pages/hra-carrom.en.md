Carrom is a game for two or four players. You flick the striker to pocket your pieces in the corners of the board.

It most likely comes from India. It has been played across the Indian subcontinent for a hundred years, and in Europe and America for the last fifty. Tournaments follow the rules of the ICF, the international federation founded in 1988.

## Rules in a nutshell

1. **Whoever begins has white.** The player who begins breaks and plays the white pieces, the opponent black. The other player begins the next board.
2. **Flick the striker.** Place the striker on your baseline and flick it at the pieces.
3. **Pocket your pieces.** Pocket one and you play again. Whoever clears their colour first wins the board.
4. **Cover the queen.** The red queen only counts if you pocket one of your own pieces straight after.
5. **Mind the fouls.** Pocket the striker and one of your pieces goes back on the board.
